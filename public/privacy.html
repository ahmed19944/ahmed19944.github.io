Privacy Policy

Spot the Difference · Android package com.std.spotthedifference
Effective 24 August 2026 · Developer: Ahmed Abd El-Hafez (STD Games)

This policy explains what Spot the Difference does with data. The short version: the game itself keeps nothing about you, and the only information leaving your device is what Google's advertising and billing services collect in order to show ads and process a purchase. There are no accounts, no sign-in, and no server of ours that stores anything.

Information the game collects

None. Your progress — which levels are finished and which differences you have found — is written to a save file in the app's private storage on your own device. It is never uploaded, and Android deletes it when you uninstall the app.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version of the game shows interstitial ads between levels and an optional rewarded ad in exchange for a hint. These are served by the Google Mobile Ads SDK, which collects and processes data independently of us, including:

  • your device's advertising ID (a resettable identifier provided by Android);
  • device and app information — model, operating system version, language, app version;
  • approximate location derived from your IP address, at roughly city level;
  • ad interactions — which ads were shown, viewed, or clicked.

Google uses this to select and measure ads, to limit how often the same ad is repeated, and to detect fraud. We receive only aggregate reporting from Google — impression and revenue totals, never information about an individual person or device. How Google handles the data is described in the Google Privacy Policy and in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

Ad personalisation is controlled from your device rather than from inside the game: on Android, under Settings → Google → Ads, you can reset or delete your advertising ID and opt out of personalised advertising. You can also review your settings at adssettings.google.com.

Purchases

The game offers one optional in-app purchase, Remove Ads. Payment is handled entirely by Google Play Billing. We never see or receive your payment details — no card number, no billing address, no name. The app asks Google Play only whether this Google account owns the purchase, so it knows whether to stop showing ads. That is why the purchase restores itself automatically after a reinstall.

Other games we make

The main menu can show a small card pointing at another game. To do that the app downloads one static file over HTTPS from ahmed19944.github.io. Nothing about you is sent with that request and no identifier is attached to it; as with any request to any website, the hosting provider (GitHub) may record the originating IP address in its own server logs. The file is a plain list of games and store links, identical for everyone.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect information from them. Ad content is capped at a Teen maturity rating, and the app is not configured for child-directed treatment. If you believe a child has provided information through the app, contact us and we will look into it.

Sharing

We do not sell your data and we do not share it with anyone beyond the Google services described above — AdMob for advertising and Google Play for billing. There are no analytics SDKs, no social network SDKs, and no third-party trackers in the app.

Retention and deletion

We hold no personal data, so there is nothing on our side to retain or delete. To remove everything the app has stored, uninstall it — that deletes the local save. To reset the identifier used for advertising, use the Android setting above. Data held by Google is subject to Google's own retention policies and can be managed through your Google account.

Other platforms

Desktop versions of Spot the Difference show no ads and contain no billing; apart from the cross-promotion file described above, they make no network requests at all.

Changes

If this policy changes, the revised version is posted at this address with a new effective date. Material changes will also be noted in the app's store listing release notes.
